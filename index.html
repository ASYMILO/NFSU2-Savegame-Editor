Click or drag a column, or focus the graph and use the arrow keys (Shift for bigger steps). Like the game's tuning screen, raising a point also lifts its direct neighbours and lowers the points further away, so the curve's total torque stays the same.

Cheat limits: each point is saved on its own, which the game's screen never does. On load, the game restores total engine torque with an equal offset at every RPM; this appears in ECU, not the turbo component. Lowering some points can push others past their maximum.

"In game" marks read the final loaded ECU or turbo component, including unique parts. ECU's equal load correction moves its marks in parallel, not proportionally; turbo receives no such correction. The result table uses combined engine torque.
